The SaaS dashboard template (`zengin create --template saas`), and what it brought into Zengin UI: `LineChart`, `BarChart` and `Sparkline` drawn in SVG on the tone tokens, the motion presets in `motion.css`, the breakpoint tokens, and `align` on `Button`. The registry ships every `src/internal` helper as a lib item and records template sources.
