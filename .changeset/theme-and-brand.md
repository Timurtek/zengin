`zengin theme [name]` lists the registry's themes or swaps the project's brand for one; `zengin create --theme` applies one at creation. `zengin brand --name <name> [--logo] [--primary] [--font-*] [--radius]` derives a whole brand from one color in OKLCH, pushed to WCAG AA in both schemes, with the logo, favicon, wordmark and index.html patched. Zengin UI ships four themes: default, meadow, plex, spec-sheet.
