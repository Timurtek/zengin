Three more templates in the registry: `auth` (sign in, create account, reset and verify, validated against mock accounts), `docs` (sections in a sidebar, markdown pages with code and tables, an outline, search, previous and next, per-page metadata from mock rows) and `storefront` (a product grid with search, filters and sort, a cart sheet with quantities and totals, a checkout dialog; stock, ratings and sale flags from mock rows). Each ships its `mock.json` and gets a `mock` script when created.
