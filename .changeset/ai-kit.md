The AI kit: `Conversation`, `Message`, `Markdown`, `CodeBlock`, `PromptInput`, `Reasoning`, `ToolCall`, `Sources`, `Suggestions`, `Loader`, in the shape of AI Elements and taking the Vercel AI SDK's message parts as they are. The `chat` template (`zengin create --template chat`) wires them with `useChat` and a scripted transport. Registry lib items are now named `lib-<name>`.
